Habit tracker: how to put it on your phone

The app is a small website that installs onto your home screen. Once it's installed it opens like any other app and works without internet. It needs to live at a web address once, so your phone can download it. GitHub Pages is free and keeps it there.

Do steps 1 to 6 on a laptop. Unzip this folder first.

1. Make a free account at github.com if you don't have one.
2. Tap the + at the top right, choose "New repository", call it habits, set it to Public, and click "Create repository".
3. On the next page, click "uploading an existing file". Drag in everything from this folder: index.html, manifest.json, sw.js and the icons folder. Click "Commit changes".
4. Go to Settings, then Pages. Under "Branch", choose main and / (root), then click Save.
5. Wait a minute or two, then refresh the page. Your address shows at the top, something like https://yourname.github.io/habits/
6. Send that address to your phone.

Then on your phone:

iPhone: open the address in Safari, tap the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
Android: open it in Chrome, tap the three dots, then "Install app" or "Add to home screen".

Open it once while you're online. After that it works offline.

Your ticks
- They're saved on your phone, inside the app. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- Under Backup, tap "Download backup" or "Copy backup" every week or two, and keep the file or text somewhere safe.
- To bring over the ticks from the Claude version, open it on claude.ai, tap "Copy backup", then in the app paste it under Backup and tap "Check this backup".
